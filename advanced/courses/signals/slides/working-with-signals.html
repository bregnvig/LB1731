Working with signals

reactive Primitives

Writable Signals: Writable Signals allow you to modify the state and notify the Consumers when changes occur. Effects: (not exactly "producers" in the traditional sense). Effects can considered as a way to produce *side effects* in response to changes in state, such as logging or triggering other actions. Consumers: Computed Signals: Consumers are components or parts of your application that depend on state values. Computed Signals are a type of Consumer. They derive their values from other Signals and automatically update when the underlying state changes. Computed Signals are consumers because they depend on state and react to changes in it. Angular Components and Templates: In Angular applications, components and templates can also be considered as consumers. They use state values, often provided by Computed Signals or directly from Writable Signals, to display information and update the user interface. When the state changes, these consumers react to it by re-rendering or updating their views.
