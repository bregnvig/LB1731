What is an operator?

An operator is just an observable. What distinguishes operators from other observables? Operators are observables that, as the name suggests, operate on a source observable.

 Netanel Basal - Creating Custom Operators in RxJS

Operators are a CORE concept in RxJS. They're special observables that work on a source observable to transform or manage its emitted values. Understanding operators is key before diving into custom operators, which allow you to create reusable, domain-specific transformations tailored to your application's needs.
