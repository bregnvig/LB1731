Leaks in Angular apps

  • A leak: something long-lived still points at your component
  • Suspects: setInterval, window listeners, a 3rd party library (like our leaflet map)
  • A long-lived manual subscribe

Destroyed cards, still in memory

Demo: /performance/leaks. Create the playground card, Destroy, three times: Created 3, Still alive 3; Memory -> Collect garbage: still 3. Reload, tick Use the fix, Create / Destroy three times, collect garbage: Created 3, Collected 3, Still alive 0. Click: cards nobody can see. Garbage collection frees what nothing points to. A leak is when something that lives longer than your component still points at it. In a single page app the page lives for hours, so every navigation can add one more. The usual suspects. setInterval: the browser holds your callback, and the callback holds the component. Listeners you put on window or document yourself. Third-party objects like a Leaflet map, which adds its own listener on window. And a subscribe you made by hand to something that outlives the component. Let me show you. /performance/leaks. Create the card, destroy it, three times. Created 3, Still alive 3. Open the Memory tab and click Collect garbage: still 3. Nothing lets go of them. Reload, tick Use the fix, same clicks, collect garbage: Created 10, Collected 10, Still alive 0. Click. Three cards destroyed, three still in memory. Two of these suspects are in our app on purpose.
