Ask for their numbers first. Before (zone.js): one list click = 46 bars in 3 s (46, 47, 46). Zoneless + signal, Eager kept: 5 bars in 3 s = 2 from the click + 3 timer bars, every bar still shows 6 components / 133 rows in the Profiler. 10 quiet s: still 10 bars, each shows 6 / 133. Dev build: the timer bar's real pass refreshes only the footer; the other 5 are the dev-only checkNoChanges pass re-running every Eager template (MEASURED.md, 2026-11-03). Welcome back. Ask a group for their list click before and after going zoneless. Click. Under zone.js one click gave us 46 bars, because Leaflet's pan animation ran inside the zone. Zoneless, the click makes two bars, plus the footer's one per second. Nobody wrapped anything: there is no zone left to patch the animation. That's the exercise 1 bonus, for free. Click. But the Profiler still shows all six components and 133 rows on every bar, the footer tick too. Ask the room: why? Every component still says Eager. On a click that is real: Angular renders all 133 rows again. On the timer tick it is partly the dev build: the real pass already refreshes only the footer, but in development Angular runs a second checking pass after every cycle, and that pass re-runs every Eager template. The Profiler shows both. In production you would not see it, but you also cannot profile there. Click. So zoneless decides when a cycle runs. Eager versus OnPush decides how much of the tree it checks. That's why the Eager lines had to go.
