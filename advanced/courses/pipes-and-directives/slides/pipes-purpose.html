Pipes - purpose

Pipes works within templates

Use pipes to style data

A pipe takes at least one value as input and transforms it to a desired output

<p>Today is {{ today }}</p>
<!-- Today is Sat Jan 4 2021 08:39:44 GMT+0200 (CEST) -->
<p>Today is {{ today | date }}</p>
<!-- Today is Jan 4, 2021 -->
Tip - You can provide a pipe, as you would any service, and then have it injected into a component. This is not the intent of pipes though. formatDate vs. DatePipe
