The form group

  • A group with an enumerated set of keys as abstract controls 
  • Use this, to group an entire form or a set of related controls. (If controls are related, groupable and reusable consider creating custom control)
  • Attach the group to the template using the formGroup or formGroupName directive 
  • Validators has access to all controls in the group. This enables validation across controls in the group
<form [formGroup]="fg">
  <!-- Rest of your controls goes here -->
</form>
We always just call the out most form group for fg
