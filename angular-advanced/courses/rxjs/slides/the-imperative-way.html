Imperative code is characterized by explicit control flow, manual iteration, and direct manipulation of state: Imperative programming tells the program exactly what to do and in what order by: focusing on explicitly describing how to achieve a result by providing step-by-step instructions. Declarative programming delegates the "how" to underlying systems or abstractions by: focusing on describing what the desired outcome should be, without specifying the exact steps to achieve it.
