WHY TEST AT ALL?

Testing can feel...: "many developers have had frustrating experiences with testing", "if testing wasn't done properly or the goals weren't clear, it often ends up feeling like extra work that yields no benefit" - "Maybe you've spent hours writing tests that broke with every little change—testing felt like it was fighting you, not helping you" "these frustrations are common, but they often stem from a lack of understanding or using the wrong approach" Easy to understand...: "Testing sounds great on paper—everyone agrees that ensuring quality is a good thing" - "However, the challenge lies in proving the value of testing, especially when dealing with tight deadlines or skeptical team members" "seeing the benefits of testing takes time, and without seeing immediate results, it can be hard to justify the upfront effort" "It’s like exercising—you know it’s good for you, but seeing the results takes consistency." Quality testing skills...: "reading about testing or watching tutorials is just the start." "Quality testing requires hands-on experience—learning how to design effective test cases, understand edge cases, and find the right balance between coverage and maintainability" " a skill that will grow over time."
