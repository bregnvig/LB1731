Unit testing

Leverage automated testing

Unit testing, one day, four exercises on the playground app. Plan: why, the unit of work, TDD, Vitest. Then exercise 1 and 2 before lunch. Today you write tests first, watch them fail, and then make them pass. We start with twenty minutes of ideas: why a test earns its place, what a unit is, the red-green-refactor loop and the shape of a Vitest spec. Then we spend most of the day in the playground app, writing specs for a filter function, a service, an HTTP call and a component.
