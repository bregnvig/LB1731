By template

What happens without the relativeTo? It goes to its own root. In this case http://localhost:4201/routing;row=1 Notice routerLink is relative by default, where the router.navigate is absolute by default (See recap-component) Show the difference in the demo between Ctrl+click with routerLink vs. router.navigate - therefor prefer the routerLink. You can create the parameterLinkArray in the component, if it gets messy in the template
