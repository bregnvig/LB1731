Filter Detached, then a class name. Click: Retainers is the chain that holds it. Follow it to the first thing you wrote. Count, don't guess. In the class filter type Detached. A detached node is a DOM node that's no longer in the page but still in memory, because something points at it. Then type a class name, like FooterComponent, and count. One footer on screen should be one FooterComponent in memory. Anything more is a leak. Click. Click one of them and look at the Retainers pane: it's the chain of references that keeps it alive. Follow it until you hit something you wrote, or a library you called. That's the pointer you need to cut.
