Signals in the template and toSignal clean up by themselves

A leak: something still references an object you no longer need, so GC can't free it. Usual suspects: intervals and timers, listeners on window/document, third-party objects, manual subscribe, long-lived services holding components or elements. The footer's setInterval and Leaflet's map are both in this app. Signals: toSignal cleans up by itself (good). effect and toSignal are eager (bad). Garbage collection frees what nothing points to. A leak is when something still points at an object you are done with. In a single page app the page lives for hours, so leaks pile up each time you navigate. The usual suspects. setInterval and setTimeout: the browser holds your callback, and the callback holds the component. Listeners you add to window or document yourself. Third-party objects like a Leaflet map: it adds listeners to the window and the DOM and holds nodes until you call remove. Subscriptions you made by hand to something that outlives the component. And services that keep a reference to a component or element. Two of these are in our app on purpose: the footer starts an interval and never clears it, and the map is never removed. The signals story is two sided. toSignal and the template do their own cleanup, there is nothing to forget. But effect and toSignal are eager, they run and subscribe even if nothing reads them, which costs. Next we find them with a heap snapshot, and then fix them.
