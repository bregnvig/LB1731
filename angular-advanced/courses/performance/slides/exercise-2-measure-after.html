Exercise 2 - Measure after

  1. Record 10 quiet seconds
  2. Count the bars, click one: how many components?
  3. Record one click on a playground in the list, 3 s
  4. Count the bars, click each
  5. Write it all down next to your before numbers

You should see: the counter still counts. Done? Come back to the room.

The same three measurements as at the start of the exercise, plus clicking each bar. Helping a group: they compare three things with their first numbers: bars in 10 quiet s and what one bar checks, and bars for one list click. Our numbers are in the debrief, not here.
