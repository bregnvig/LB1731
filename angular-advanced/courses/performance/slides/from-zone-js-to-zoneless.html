Recap from signals. Guesses vs is told. runOutsideAngular from exercise 1 can stay: it still compiles and does nothing harmful zoneless. You saw this slide in the signals course, so one minute. Zone.js guesses: an event or a timer finished, so maybe something changed, check. Zoneless is told: a signal a template reads changed, so refresh that view. New apps since v21 are zoneless and write nothing in main.ts. Our app still says provideZoneChangeDetection. In exercise 2 you swap it for provideZonelessChangeDetection and take zone.js out of the build. Your runOutsideAngular from exercise 1 can stay.
