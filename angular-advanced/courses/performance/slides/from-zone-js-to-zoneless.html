Recap from signals. Guesses vs is told. Versions: zoneless stable in v20, default in v21. runOutsideAngular from exercise 1 can stay: it still compiles and does no harm zoneless. You saw this in the signals course, so one minute. zone.js guesses: an event or a timer finished, so maybe something changed, check. Zoneless is told: a signal a template reads changed, so refresh that view. Zoneless became stable in Angular 20 and is the default for new apps since 21: they write nothing in main.ts. Our app still says provideZoneChangeDetection. In exercise 2 you swap it for provideZonelessChangeDetection and take zone.js out of the build. Your runOutsideAngular from exercise 1 can stay.
