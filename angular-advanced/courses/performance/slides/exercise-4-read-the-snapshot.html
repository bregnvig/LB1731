There's no single "Detached" total in DevTools: one row per tag. Read the leaflet row. In snapshot two, type Detached in the class filter, and you'll see one row per tag: Detached div, Detached button, and so on. DevTools has no total for all of them, so read one row that means something: Detached <leaflet>. Write the count down. Then type FooterComponent and write that count down too. Then open the Detached <leaflet> row, click one of them and look at the Retainers pane underneath: the chain of references that keeps it alive. Follow it until you hit something you wrote, or a library you called. Ask the room to guess before they look. The trap: if you click around in the Elements panel or log a node to the console, DevTools itself holds on to it, and it shows up with DevTools console as its retainer. That's not your app. Clear the console and take the snapshot from a fresh tab.
