On the right is what that looks like: two snapshots in the sidebar, and a class filter at the top. Why do I insist on counting rounds? Because you'll count footers in a minute, and the number will surprise you if you don't know this. Look at app.routes.ts: the empty path and ':id' are two route entries with the same HomeComponent. Selecting a playground goes from one to the other, and the router builds a new Home. Tilbage til kortet goes back to the empty path: another new Home. Two per round. Five rounds, ten Homes that should be gone. Ask the room: the screenshot says FooterComponent ×11. Who expected 1?
