Bonus, not in the solution: exercise 2 fixes it for the whole app. Record one list click, wrap setZoom/setView in runOutsideAngular, record again. For the fast ones. Click a playground in the list and record it: you get a burst of bars, not one. The click itself is one cycle, but the two center$ subscriptions call setZoom and setView inside the zone, and Leaflet animates the pan from there: every animation frame is a patched task. Wrap those two calls in runOutsideAngular, the same trick, and record the same click again. Compare the two counts. It's not in the solution, on purpose: in exercise 2 we take zone.js out, and then this problem is gone for the whole app at once.
