Performance & Change Detection

Measure, change, measure again

Performance & Change Detection: one day, four labs on the playground app. Ex 1 leave the zone, ex 2 zoneless and signals, ex 3 load less, ex 4 memory. Rule of the day: measure, change, measure again. Welcome. Today we make the playground app faster, and we only believe numbers. The app is slow in ways you can see in a profiler. In every lab you measure first, change one thing, and measure again to see the number move. Four labs: take the map out of the zone, go zoneless with signals, load less up front, and clean up what leaks. Ask the room who has opened a profiler on their own app. That tells you how long to stay on the tools slides.
