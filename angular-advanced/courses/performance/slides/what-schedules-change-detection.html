The demo still runs zone.js: setTimeout on B does start a cycle, OnPush just skips B. Zoneless, no cycle would start at all. Click: the rows zoneless does NOT schedule. Click: the rule. Dropped row, say it: assigning a plain field never scheduled anything, not even with zone.js. The next cycle just happened to show it. Here's the list to keep in your head. Top three: a signal read in a template, a listener in a template, markForCheck, which the async pipe calls for you. Those schedule a cycle with or without Zone.js. Click. The bottom two used to work only because the zone ran a cycle after every timer and every finished request. Zoneless, they schedule nothing. And assigning a plain field never scheduled anything, the next cycle just happened to pick it up.
