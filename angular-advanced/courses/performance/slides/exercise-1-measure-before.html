Exercise 1 - Measure before

  1. Wait for the list. DevTools, Angular tab, Profiler, Record
  2. Move the mouse slowly over the map, no clicks. Stop after exactly 5 seconds
  3. Count the bars. Write down your count
  4. Click one bar: which components were checked?

You should see: new bars while the mouse moves. No Angular tab? Plan B: ng.enableProfiling() in the Console, then Performance, Record.

Exactly 5 s, mouse moving slowly over the map, no clicks. Count, write it down, click one bar. Helping a group: no bars at all usually means they recorded before the list loaded, or on the wrong tab. A fixed 5 s is what makes before and after comparable, so make them redo a sloppy recording. Don't give them our number; it's in the debrief.
