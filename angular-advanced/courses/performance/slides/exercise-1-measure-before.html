Exercise 1 - Measure before

Wait for the list. Open DevTools, Angular, Profiler.

  1. Press Record
  2. Move the mouse slowly over the map, no clicks. Record exactly 5 seconds
  3. Press Stop and count the bars. Write the number down
  4. Click one bar: which components were refreshed?

Nothing on the page reads the mouse. The map is Leaflet, created inside Angular's zone, so zone.js runs a cycle after every one of its events.
