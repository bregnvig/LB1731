Wait until the list is there, open DevTools, the Angular tab, Profiler, press Record. Move the mouse slowly over the map, no clicks, no zoom, and stop after exactly five seconds. A fixed time is what makes before and after comparable. Every bar in the Profiler is one change detection cycle. Count them and write your number down. Then click one of the bars: the Profiler lists the components that were checked in that cycle and how long each took. Don't say a number yet. Let them count, then the next slide but one has ours.
