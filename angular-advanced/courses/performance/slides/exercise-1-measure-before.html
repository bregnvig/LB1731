Exercise 1 - Measure before

Lab labs/performance/exercise-1, app running with npm start.

  1. Wait for the spinner to go away. Open DevTools > Angular > Profiler
  2. Press Record. Move the mouse over the map for 5 seconds, drag it, zoom with the wheel. Press Stop
  3. Count the bars. Write the number down

What you should see: a lot of bars, one for every mouse move, drag and zoom step, plus one bar every second. Click a bar: the whole app was checked, even FooterComponent.

The every-second bars come from the footer: Opdateret for N sekunder siden. We will not fix those in this exercise.

Profiler: Record, 5 s of mouse over the map, drag, zoom, Stop. Write down the bar count. Expect: a bar per mousemove + one per second (footer). Why? Leaflet registers its listeners inside the zone -> zone.js runs CD for the whole app after each one. Measure before. Open Angular DevTools, the Profiler tab, press Record. Now move the mouse over the map for about five seconds, drag it, zoom with the wheel. Press Stop. You get a forest of bars. Roughly one per mouse move, and in the quiet moments one per second. Write down how many bars you got for your five seconds. Click a few. Every one of them checked the whole app: the sidebar with its 133 rows, the footer, everything, because a mouse moved over a map. In our own run, 60 mouse moves gave 61 bars, and every bar refreshed all six components on the page and all 133 rows. Why? Leaflet adds its mousemove, drag and zoom listeners when it creates the map. We create the map inside Angular's zone, so zone.js patches those listeners and runs change detection after each. Nothing in the app reads a mouse move on the map. Next slide: how to stop that.
