In your app: leaving the zone

  1. Profile your slowest screen for 5 s
  2. In the "Bottom up" view, notice what started it all.
  3. Determine if the event is needed at all for you Angular app to work. Any 3rd party libs?
  4. Wrap them in runOutsideAngular, measure again. Are there fewer change detections? Are the databindings still working?
  5. Are the databindings not working anymore? Step back inside the zone.
