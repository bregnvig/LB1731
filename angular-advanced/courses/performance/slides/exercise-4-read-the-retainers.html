Exercise 4 - Read the retainers

Click a detached node and read Retainers. What keeps the old Home alive?

Window
  → _leaflet_events
  → resize handler
  → L.Map
  → _container
  → div.leaflet-container
  → <leaflet>
  → <main>
  → ... the whole old Home

Trap: anything you selected in Elements ($0) or logged to the console is kept alive under DevTools console. Ignore those.

Retainers on a detached node: Window -> _leaflet_events -> resize handler -> L.Map -> ... -> <main>. Trap: DevTools console retainers. Now click one of the detached nodes and read the Retainers pane. You'll see a chain like the one on the right: it starts at Window, goes through _leaflet_events, a resize handler, the Leaflet map, its container div, and from there up through the leaflet element and main into the whole old Home. Ask the room: who put a listener on window? Let them guess before you click. The trap: if you click around in the Elements panel or log a node to the console, DevTools itself holds on to it, and it shows up with DevTools console as its retainer. That's not your app. Ignore those.
