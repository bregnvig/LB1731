Exercise 4 - Read the retainers

Click a detached node and read Retainers. What keeps the old Home alive?

Trap: anything you selected in Elements ($0) or logged to the console is kept alive under DevTools console. Ignore those entries.

Window

Leaflet's resize listener on window: only map.remove() takes it off.

The footer's setInterval callback holds the rest: nobody calls clearInterval.

Retainers on a detached node: Window -> _leaflet_events -> resize handler -> L.Map -> ... -> <main>. Click: the two culprits. Trap: DevTools console retainers. Now click one of the detached nodes and read the Retainers pane. You'll see a chain like the one on the right: it starts at Window, goes through _leaflet_events, a resize handler, the Leaflet map, its container div, and from there up through the leaflet element and main into the whole old Home. Ask the room: who put a listener on window? Let them guess before you click. Click. Leaflet did. A map listens for window resize, and only map.remove() takes the listener off. Nobody calls it, so the map keeps its container, and a detached node keeps its parents, so the whole old Home stays. The footer is the other half: the setInterval callback still runs every second and holds the footer. The trap: if you click around in the Elements panel or log a node to the console, DevTools itself holds on to it, and it shows up with DevTools console as its retainer. That's not your app. Ignore those.
