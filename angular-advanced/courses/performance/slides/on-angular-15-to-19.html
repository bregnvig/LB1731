For everyone not on 22 yet. Before v22 OnPush was opt-in per component. Signals from v16 (stable in v17). Zoneless stable in v20, default for new apps in v21. Not everyone here is on Angular 22 at work, so here's today in older versions. Click. OnPush exists in every version, but before 22 you opt in, one component at a time: changeDetection: ChangeDetectionStrategy.OnPush. Start with the leaves that render a lot, like list rows. Click. Then the component has to be told when to update: the async pipe, markForCheck, and from Angular 16 signals in the template. Click. Your app runs zone.js, so runOutsideAngular from exercise 1 is the cheapest win you have: it works in every version. Click. Zoneless needs Angular 20 to be stable, and 21 makes it the default for new apps. If you're on 19, it's still experimental there. Click. Same ideas as today, you just opt in instead of getting them by default. Ask the room: which version is your app on?
