  • For thousands of rows. 133 rows: not worth it

Only if asked. @angular/cdk is not a dependency in these labs: name it, no lab. The strongest answer to a long list is not to render it. Virtual scrolling keeps only the rows that are visible, and a few around them, in the DOM, and swaps them as you scroll. It comes from the CDK and needs fixed or estimated row heights. For 133 rows it's more machinery than it's worth. Think of it from a few thousand rows.
