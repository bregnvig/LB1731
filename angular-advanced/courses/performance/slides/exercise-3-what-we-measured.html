Punchline: the obvious suspect (Leaflet) is not the biggest win. Measure to find it. Numbers measured on step-3 in a scratch copy; theirs differ a little. Let's compare. On step-3 I applied the changes one at a time. Registering only the spinner icon takes the initial download from 1.77 megabytes down to 761 kilobytes, from 383 to 161 kilobytes transferred. That is the biggest single win, and the change is two lines. Those two icon packs are most of main. Deferring the map alone only goes to 1.63 megabytes. Leaflet is 155 kilobytes. It is the obvious suspect, it is the library everyone points at, and it is not where most of the weight is. All the changes together: 621 kilobytes, 126 transferred. The shared chunk is Angular itself, which now gets split out because there are lazy chunks. So the lesson: do not guess where the weight is. Measure, then change the biggest thing first.
