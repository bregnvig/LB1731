Change 4: main.ts. Delete initializeFontAwesomeFactory and the fas/far imports; provideAppInitializer(() => inject(FaIconLibrary).addIcons(faSpinner)). Forget addIcons: no spinner, console "Could not find icon with iconName=spinner and prefix=fas in the icon library." The icons, the thing the analyzer showed you. Open main.ts. The app registers every icon in two FontAwesome packs, solid and regular, through initializeFontAwesomeFactory. The app shows one: the spinner. So delete the factory and the two pack imports, import faSpinner, and in provideAppInitializer call addIcons with just that one. If you remove the packs and forget addIcons, the spinners disappear and the console tells you it can't find the spinner icon. Build, write the number down. Then the next slide: watch the chunks load.
