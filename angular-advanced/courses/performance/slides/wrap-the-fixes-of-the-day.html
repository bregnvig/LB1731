Four fixes, in the order they paid off in THIS app. Yours will differ: measure. Read the Monday line out. Click: the question. Here's the day on one slide, in the order they paid off in our app. One: load less. Our first download was 1.77 megabytes, and the biggest part wasn't the map, it was an icon library we registered in full. The analyzer showed it. Only the icon we use, a lazy route and a deferred map, and Initial total was 620 kilobytes. On a slow phone, first paint went from 4.4 to 2.3 seconds. @defer needs Angular 17. Two: the map's mouse events ran change detection for the whole app. Five seconds of mouse moves, about 300 Profiler bars, 229 milliseconds of change detection on a slow CPU. One runOutsideAngular, and it was 5 bars and 6 milliseconds. Works in every version that has zone.js. Three: every visit left the old page's DOM and a footer behind. After five rounds, ten detached maps in the heap snapshot. Two onDestroy callbacks, and it was 0. DestroyRef needs Angular 16. Four: zoneless and signals. When the footer ticks, the Profiler shows Angular checking the footer instead of all six components, and the list click went from 46 bars to 2. Stable from Angular 20. It pays more the bigger your component tree is. So on Monday: in your own app, start with the kind of slow your users complain about, not the fix you liked best today. Measure that first: Initial total from the build, bars in the Profiler, or counts across two heap snapshots. Write the number down, change one thing, measure again. Click.
