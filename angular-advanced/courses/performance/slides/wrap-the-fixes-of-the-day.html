The fixes of the day

Load less: 1.77 MB → 621 kB

runOutsideAngular: ~290 → 5 bars in 5 s

DestroyRef.onDestroy: 10 leaked footers → 0

Zoneless + signals: 6 → 1 component per tick

Which one would you try at work?

Four fixes, in the order they paid off in THIS app. Yours will differ: measure. Read the Monday line out. Click: the question to the room. Here's the day on one slide, in the order they paid off in our app. One: load less. Our first download was 1.77 megabytes, and the biggest part wasn't the map, it was an icon library we registered in full. Only the icons we use, a lazy route with loadComponent and two defer blocks, and Initial total in the build output was 621 kilobytes. Every user pays that on every load, so it goes first. Two: the map's mouse events ran change detection for the whole app. Five seconds of mouse moves, about 290 Profiler bars. One runOutsideAngular, and it was 5: one per second, the footer. Three: every visit left a footer and a map behind. After five rounds that was 10 footers and 10 detached maps, Detached <leaflet> ×10, in the heap snapshot. Two onDestroy callbacks, and it was 0. Small per visit, but it grows for as long as the tab is open. Four: zoneless and signals. Here the honest number is smaller: when the footer ticks, the Profiler shows Angular refreshing the footer instead of all six components. A click in the list still refreshes everything once. It pays more the bigger your component tree is. So on Monday: in your own app, start with the kind of slow your users complain about, not with the fix you liked best today. Measure that first: Initial total from the build, bars in the Profiler, or instances across two heap snapshots. Write the number down, change one thing, measure again. Click.
