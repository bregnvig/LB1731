In your app: the first download

  1. npm run build. Notice your Initial total
  2. Analyzer: What are the three biggest things in main
  3. Loading entire libraries? Only import what you need (e.g. fa-spinner)
  4. Rarely visited route or feature? loadComponent / loadChildren
  5. Heavy, not needed at first paint? @defer it
