linkedSignal - keep the selection

The long form gets the previous value

Keep the selection if it is still in the new list, otherwise take the first

selected = linkedSignal<Playground[], Playground>({
  source: this.playgrounds,
  computation: (playgrounds, previous) =>
    playgrounds.find(p => p.id === previous?.value.id)
      ?? playgrounds[0],
});
Demo: /signals/linked. Select random, then Shuffle (same playgrounds, new order): left (short form) jumps to first, right (long form) keeps the selection. New playgrounds resets both.
