keyValue pipe

Used for iterating over Records and Maps

Keys are sorted alphabetically by default

  myObject = {
    a: 'A is the first letter',
    b: 'B is the second',
  };

  myMap = new Map<string, string>([
    ['b', 'B is the second'],
    ['a', 'A is the first letter'],
  ]);
