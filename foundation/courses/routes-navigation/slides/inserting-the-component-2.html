Inserting the component

Each route that has children, also have a <router-outlet>

E.g the app component’s template

<nav>
    <!-- Here we have the navigation bar visible on all pages -->
</nav>
<div>
   <router-outlet/>
   <app-orders>
      <ul>
         ...
      </ul>
      <router-outlet/>
      <app-order/>
   </app-orders>
</div>
